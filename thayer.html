Thayer Infill Site
Completion Date
Summer 2021
Site Address
224 S Thayer St, Ann Arbor, MI 48104
Data Type
Photogrammetry, LiDAR
Scan Type
Exterior

The Thayer Infill Site project explores how immersive technologies can expand the architectural model beyond its physical constraints, offering new ways to engage with scale, context, and community. Traditionally, physical models have served as essential tools in architectural practice, used to test formal and compositional ideas at a reduced scale. However, their limited size often restricts our ability to fully understand how a design might be experienced in the real world.

To address this, EIPC developed a prototype workflow that reimagines the model as a site-specific, full-scale digital environment. The process begins with a photogrammetry scan of a physical white museum board model, which is then scaled up and integrated into a LiDAR scan of the Thayer Street site. The composite environment is imported into Unreal Engine, where it can be navigated through VR headsets or within the UMich VR Cave, enabling designers to inhabit and evaluate their proposals at 1:1 scale.

This immersive experience enables deeper design testing, not only in terms of spatial logic and proportion, but also concerning the existing urban fabric and neighboring architecture. Designers can walk through their proposals as if built, considering how their interventions relate to the lives, rhythms, and textures of the surrounding community.
