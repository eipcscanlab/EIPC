Eda U. Gerstacker Grove
Completion Date
Fall 2022
Site Address
1230 Murfin, Ann Arbor, MI 48109
Data Type
LiDAR
Scan Type
Exterior

EIPC digitally documented the Eda U. Gerstacker Grove, a central green space on North Campus designed to support student gathering, outdoor events, and campus connectivity. The scan captured the grove’s sculptural landscape features, integrated lighting, and circulation paths, providing a high-resolution record for planning, analysis, and archival purposes.
