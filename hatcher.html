EIPC conducted a 3D scanning project on the 8th floor of the Hatcher Graduate Library at the University of Michigan, aiming to document a series of administrative offices for reference and planning purposes. While the scan captured the general spatial layout, the abundance of glass partitions and reflective surfaces posed significant challenges for data accuracy and completeness—highlighting the technical limitations of reality capture in environments dominated by transparent and reflective materials. The project served as a valuable case study in assessing scanner performance in complex interior conditions and informed adjustments for future workflows in similarly glazed architectural contexts.
